# Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This app streams music. This page says what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

## What we collect

- **Your Google account name, e-mail and picture**, when you sign in, to identify your account.
- **What you play and save**: track, how long you listened, when, and at what quality. We use it to show your
  listening stats, recommend music, and calculate what each artist is paid.
- **Your subscription status** from Google Play. We never see your card or UPI details; Google handles payment.
- **The genres and artists you pick** when you start.

## What we do not collect

- **Microphone audio** is recorded only when you tap Recognize, for 8 seconds. It is sent once to match the song
  and then discarded. It is never stored. If the song is not in our catalog and outside recognition is switched on,
  the same clip is sent once to our recognition provider (AudD) and not stored by us.
- No contacts, location, advertising ID or files outside the app.

## Downloads

Downloaded music is encrypted and stored only inside the app on your phone. It is excluded from phone backups.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Artists receive only totals: how much their music was played in a month.

## Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account and data. This removes your account, library, listening history and
subscription record from our servers, and your downloads from the phone. Cancel an active Google Play
subscription separately in Google Play. To ask for deletion without the app, e-mail the address on the Play listing.
